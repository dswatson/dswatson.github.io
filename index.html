I am a Senior Research Scientist at Google DeepMind. My primary research interests include information theory, AI ethics, and computational biology.

Previously, I was an Associate Professor at King's College London's Department of Informatics, where I led a lab focused on causal machine learning and tractable probabilistic inference. I completed my doctorate on explainable AI at the University of Oxford, and was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Statistical Science at University College London. I have served on the editorial boards of Philosophy & Technology and Minds & Machines.

TL;DR – I am passionate about developing rigorous, interpretable methods for analyzing complex systems in the natural and social sciences.
